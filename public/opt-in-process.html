Tracify — SMS Opt-In Process

Consent-based location sharing platform • https://tracify-geo.com • Document date: April 2026

Overview

Tracify sends individual, transactional SMS messages to specific phone numbers provided by authenticated, paying subscribers. Each SMS contains a one-time consent link. No location data is ever collected without explicit, multi-step opt-in from the SMS recipient.

Opt-In Flow (5 Steps)

1
Subscriber Pays
Creates account, purchases subscription via Stripe checkout
→
2
Enters Phone
Subscriber enters target phone number on dashboard
→
3
SMS Sent
Single SMS with consent link sent to target
→
4
Recipient Consents
Taps link, clicks "Share My Location" on consent page
→
5
Browser Prompt
Approves native browser geolocation permission

The recipient must complete all three consent actions (steps 3, 4, 5) before any data is shared. Declining at any stage stops the process entirely.

Example SMS Message

Someone you know wants to check on your location.
Click here to respond: https://tracify-geo.com/api/tracking/consent/44

Messages are 1–2 sentences plus a unique HTTPS link. Each link is single-use and expires after one response. No marketing, promotional, or bulk content is ever included.

Consent Page (What Recipient Sees)

📍

Location Sharing Request

Someone has requested to know your location using Tracify.
By clicking "Share Location", you consent to sharing your current GPS coordinates.

Share My Location Decline

After clicking "Share My Location", the browser displays a native geolocation permission prompt ("Allow this site to access your location?"). The recipient must tap "Allow" — this is a third layer of consent controlled by the device operating system.

Anti-Abuse Safeguards

  • Rate limit: Maximum 1 SMS per subscriber per 3 hours
  • Authentication required: Only logged-in, paying subscribers can send requests
  • Single-use links: Each consent link works once and then expires permanently
  • No follow-ups: Recipients who ignore or decline are never contacted again automatically
  • No bulk sending: Each SMS is individually triggered by a subscriber action
  • One-time capture: Location is recorded once (single lat/lng), no continuous tracking
  • No app required: Recipient does not need to install anything

Key Facts

Message type: Transactional (not marketing)
Volume: ~50–150 messages/week, growing to ~500–1,000/week
Recipients: Individual phone numbers of people personally known to subscribers (family, partners, friends)
Sending number: Single dedicated US number
Opt-out: Recipients can decline on the consent page or simply ignore the SMS. No recurring messages are sent.
