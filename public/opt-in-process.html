How Users Opt In

Users opt in to receive SMS messages during account signup at tracify-geo.com. Before completing registration, users must check a required checkbox that reads:

☑ "By signing up, I agree to receive SMS messages and emails from Tracify. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out."

The checkbox is required — signup cannot proceed without it. This consent is collected on all landing pages across the website.
