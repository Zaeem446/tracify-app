The Tracify method in plain English

Tracify is a phone tracker by number that works in every country on every mobile network. Unlike spyware tools that require physical access to a device, Tracify uses a privacy-first, consent-based approach: we send a custom SMS to the target number, the recipient agrees to share their location with one tap, and you get precise GPS coordinates in your dashboard.

This means Tracify is legal to use anywhere in the world — as long as the recipient gives consent by responding to the SMS. No app installation. No rooting or jailbreaking. No background agents. Just a number, a message, and a location.

Tracify in three steps

1

Enter the phone number

Type the phone number you want to locate and customise the SMS message the recipient will see. You can explain why you're asking — many users write a short reassurance like "Hey, it's Mum — can you share your location?"

2

Send the consent SMS

Tracify delivers your custom message to the target number. The recipient sees the sender's name you chose, reads your message, and decides whether to share their location. If they agree, they tap a single link to approve the request.

3

Receive the GPS location

The moment consent is given, the exact GPS coordinates appear in your dashboard on an interactive map. You can see where the phone is — down to street level — and return to check fresh locations whenever the recipient approves follow-up pings.
