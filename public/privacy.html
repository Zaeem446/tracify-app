Your Privacy Matters

We are committed to protecting your personal information.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Finalecom Solution LLC, trading as Tracify ("we", "us", or "our"), collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our website at tracify-geo.com and our phone location tracking services. By using our services, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Email address, name, and password when you register
  • Payment Information: Credit card details processed securely through a PCI-compliant payment processor
  • Phone Numbers: Your mobile number for SMS verification and numbers you submit for location tracking requests
  • Location Data: GPS coordinates shared by consenting recipients
  • Usage Data: How you interact with our service, including IP address and browser type

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our location tracking service
  • To process your payments and manage your subscription
  • To send SMS location requests to the phone numbers you provide
  • To communicate with you about your account and service updates
  • To improve our services and develop new features
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Payment Processor: Our PCI-compliant payment processor, for handling transactions securely
  • SMS Providers: To deliver location request messages
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law or to protect our rights

All third-party providers are contractually obligated to protect your data.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmission
  • Secure password hashing
  • Regular security audits
  • Limited employee access to personal data

Note: While we take extensive precautions, no internet transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@tracify-geo.com.

7. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Keep you logged into your account
  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Analyze website traffic and usage patterns
  • Improve our service performance

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some features of our service.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. After account deletion, we may retain certain data for legal compliance, dispute resolution, or legitimate business purposes for up to 90 days.

9. Children's Privacy

Tracify is not intended for use by individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover that a child has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly.

10. SMS Communications

We collect your mobile phone number to send one-time verification codes (OTP) during account registration and login. By entering your phone number on the Tracify website and clicking the verification button, you consent to receive these SMS messages.

  • Purpose: Identity verification and account security
  • Frequency: 1–5 messages per month depending on account activity
  • Opt-Out: Reply STOP to any Tracify SMS to unsubscribe
  • Help: Reply HELP or email support@tracify-geo.com
  • Rates: Standard message and data rates may apply

Your phone number is never sold, shared, or used for marketing by third parties.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on our website and updating the "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance.
