About Tracify

What is Tracify?

Tracify is a phone tracker by number — a web-based service that locates any mobile phone worldwide using only its phone number. It works by sending a consent-based SMS to the target phone. Once the recipient agrees to share their location, you receive the precise GPS coordinates in your Tracify dashboard.

Is it legal to use Tracify?

Yes. Tracify is completely legal because every tracking request requires explicit consent from the person being tracked. The recipient must actively agree to share their location by responding to the SMS. We comply with GDPR, CCPA, and other global privacy regulations.

Which carriers and networks does Tracify support?

Tracify supports every major mobile carrier worldwide. Whether the target phone uses GSM, CDMA, LTE, 4G, or 5G, our service can deliver a location request to any valid phone number in any country.

Do I need to know the phone model?

No. All you need is the phone number. You don't need to know the make, model, operating system, or carrier. Tracify handles all the technical details automatically.

Does Tracify support iOS and Android?

Yes. Tracify works with both iOS (iPhone) and Android devices. Our SMS-based consent system works with any smartphone that can receive text messages and has location services enabled.

How fast does Tracify return a location?

As soon as the SMS recipient taps the consent link, you'll see the GPS coordinates almost instantly in your dashboard. Total time depends on how quickly the recipient responds to the request.

Do I need physical access to the target phone?

Never. Tracify is 100% remote. There's nothing to install on your device or the target's device. You just enter the phone number, we send the SMS, and the recipient chooses whether to share their location.

Can I track a phone number's location without installing an app?

Yes. Tracify is a no-installation phone tracker. You don't install anything on your device or the target's device. Everything happens through a standard SMS link and a web dashboard, so you can find a location by phone number from any browser on iOS, Android, Windows, or Mac.

How do I locate a phone by number anonymously?

Tracify lets you set a custom sender name on every request, so the recipient never sees "Tracify" — they only see the name and message you choose. Combined with our encrypted dashboard, this makes Tracify an effectively anonymous phone tracker: the recipient chooses to share their location, but they never learn which service processed the request.

Can I track a phone that is turned off?

No. A phone must be switched on and connected to a mobile network to receive the consent SMS. If the device is off, airplane mode, or out of signal, the request waits in the carrier queue and delivers as soon as the phone reconnects. You'll be notified in your dashboard the moment the location is shared.

How accurate is phone tracking by number?

Once the recipient approves the request, Tracify returns the same real-time GPS coordinates the phone uses for Google Maps or Apple Maps — typically accurate to within 5-15 meters outdoors. Indoor accuracy depends on Wi-Fi and cell-tower triangulation, usually within 20-50 meters.

Can I track a phone by IMEI number?

Tracify uses the phone number, not the IMEI. IMEI tracking generally requires carrier or law-enforcement cooperation and is not available to consumers. Our consent-based SMS system is designed to be the fastest, safest legal way for an individual to locate a phone by number.

Can I track someone without their knowledge?

No. Tracify is a consent-based service and will always notify the recipient that their location is being requested. The recipient must actively tap a link to share their location. This is what keeps Tracify legal and ethical.

Privacy & Security

What data does Tracify collect about me?

Only what's required to run your account: email, country, billing records, and the phone numbers you submit for tracking. We don't sell data and we don't run third-party ad trackers on our service. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Is my data encrypted?

Yes. All traffic between your browser and Tracify is encrypted with TLS. Data at rest is encrypted and stored with a tier-1 cloud provider. Payment details never touch our servers — they're handled directly by Stripe.

Will the person I'm tracking know who sent the SMS?

You can set the sender name on each request. The recipient sees that name and your custom message. Tracify itself remains confidential from the recipient — they only see what you choose to show them.

Troubleshooting

What if the recipient ignores the SMS?

If the recipient doesn't tap the consent link, no location is shared. You can re-send the request or customise a new message. Tracking only ever happens after active consent.

My dashboard says the request failed. What happened?

Failed requests usually mean the number is invalid, the phone is off, or the carrier blocked the international SMS. Check the number format (include country code) and try again. If the issue persists, contact support.

Can I still use Tracify if the target phone is abroad?

Yes. Tracify works in every country where mobile networks exist. International phone numbers work exactly like domestic ones — as long as you include the correct country code.
